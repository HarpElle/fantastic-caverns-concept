## Status and plan (read first)

`status/CURRENT.md` says where this repo's work stands; `status/PLAN.md` is
the product plan. Read both before starting. When your work changes what is
live, in flight, or planned, update them, mirror the change in
`status/status.json`, and append a dated entry to `status/LOG.md` in the same
commit or PR. Rules are in `status/README.md`.
